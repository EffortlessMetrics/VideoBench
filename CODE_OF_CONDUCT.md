# Code of conduct

We are committed to a project environment that is useful, fair, and free from harassment.

Contributors must:

- critique work and evidence rather than personal identity;
- disclose uncertainty, conflicts, and provenance limits;
- respect privacy, copyright, consent, and professional-editor authorship;
- avoid deceptive benchmark submissions, hidden retries, fabricated receipts, and undisclosed fixture access;
- accept correction when evidence changes the result.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate exposure of private information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or retaliation against good-faith review.

Repository maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or ban contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or sensitive concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel rather than litigated in public issues.
